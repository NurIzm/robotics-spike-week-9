# Қазақша нұсқа: терминдер мен стиль

The Kazakh copy lives in `kk/` (pages) and `src/kk/` (views, template). Build it with `node src/build.js kk`, then run
`node src/kk/check.js`: it lists text that is still identical to the Russian files. The Russian files are the source:
when one changes, change its copy in `kk/js/` or `src/kk/views/` the same way.

## Style

- Kazakh Cyrillic as in current school textbooks, with ә ғ қ ң ө ұ ү һ і where the word needs them. No Latin script.
- Speak to the student as «сен», like the Russian «ты»: «Бас», «Ұста», «Жібер», «Құрастыр», «Тексер».
- Short sentences for 10–14-year-olds. Buttons and labels stay short: at most about a third longer than the Russian.
- No plural after numbers: «алты текше», «28 қадам», «2 минут».
- Quotes «…», dash —, ranges with an en dash: «6–10».
- Facts, numbers and quiz answers stay exactly as in the Russian files.
- LEGO, SPIKE, SPIKE Prime stay as they are. Canadarm2 is «Канадарм2», МКС is «ХҒС».
- The SPIKE app has no Kazakh, so students open it in Russian. Where the text names a place in the app, keep the Russian
  name in quotes: «Модели → Роборука».

## The model

The model is «Робот-қол». In the Russian SPIKE app it is called «Роборука»; say so once, in the build section.

## Blocks

Block labels follow SPIKE's style: short, lower case, the verb last as Kazakh needs. Scratch's own Kazakh strings come from
`vendor/blockly/msg_kk.js`; `kk/js/blocks.js` puts corrections on top of them. Palette categories: Моторлар, Қозғалыс,
Жарық, Дыбыс, Оқиғалар, Басқару, Датчиктер, Операторлар, Айнымалылар, Менің блоктарым, Қосымша моторлар.
